Lovable credits extension: keep your balance in view

Most credit problems are really visibility problems. You do not overspend because you wanted to; you overspend because the number was out of sight until it mattered.

This page covers the tracking side of the LAST ZONE extension specifically — what it shows, how to use it while you build, and where its limits are.

What the panel tracks

  • Your current credit balance, updating as you work.
  • The daily device switch count, so moving machines is never a surprise.
  • The server you are connected to.
  • The extension version, which matters when you contact support.

Using it during a build

  1. 1Open the panel before the first request and note the starting number.
  2. 2Run one meaningful step, then look again — that difference is your real per-step cost.
  3. 3Set a floor for the session and stop starting new work when you reach it.
  4. 4Top up before the next session rather than in the middle of one.

Spotting waste with the numbers

Once you can see cost per step, expensive habits become obvious. A full-page regeneration that costs several times a targeted fix is hard to keep doing once you have watched the balance move.

People who track for a week almost always change how they write requests, and that change saves more than the tracking itself.

Honest limits

  • It does not create, extend or discount credits.
  • It does not produce an itemised historical report.
  • It cannot recover credits already spent on a retry.
  • It needs an active license key to show anything at all.

Frequently asked questions

Does tracking itself cost credits?

No. Reading your balance is not billable work.

Will it warn me before I run out?

The number is in view while you work, so you see it falling. There is no separate alarm.

Can I track two keys at once?

One key is active in the panel at a time.
