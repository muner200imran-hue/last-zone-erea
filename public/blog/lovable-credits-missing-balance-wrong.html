Lovable Credits Missing or Balance Wrong? How to Fix Deducted Credits

LAST ZONESeptember 24, 2026

Imran bought extra credits on Friday evening for a weekend project. On Saturday morning he logged in and his balance looked lower than he expected. He had not built anything yet. Were his Lovable credits missing? Was the credit balance wrong? Had something been deducted by mistake?

This situation is stressful, especially when you have paid for credits. The good news: in most cases there is a clear explanation, and when there is a real error, support can help. This guide gives you a calm, step-by-step checklist.

The Problem: Your Numbers Do Not Match

People usually notice one of these:

  • Credits are lower than they remember
  • A purchase or top-up does not seem to appear
  • A single request deducted more than expected
  • The balance changed while they were offline
  • The balance shown in two places looks different

Let us go through the causes from most common to least common.

Step 1: Refresh and Wait a Moment

It sounds simple, but balances can take a short time to update, especially right after a purchase or a large build.

  1. Refresh the page (or sign out and back in).
  2. Wait a few minutes after a purchase.
  3. Check Settings → Plans & Credits again.

This alone solves a surprising number of cases.

Step 2: Check the Right Period

Many plans combine daily credits and monthly credits. If you look at the daily number, you might think your monthly credits are missing.

  • Check which balance you are looking at.
  • Check your reset date.
  • Remember that unused daily credits usually expire at the reset.

For details, read our guide on the Lovable free plan and daily credits.

Step 3: Check Workspace Members

Credits usually belong to the workspace, not the individual person. If you share a workspace:

  1. Open workspace members.
  2. See who has access.
  3. Ask teammates whether they worked recently.

This is one of the most common reasons for "missing" credits. Remove anyone who no longer needs access.

Step 4: Read Your Credit History

Your credit history shows exactly when credits were used.

  1. Open the usage or history view.
  2. Find the time when the balance dropped.
  3. Open the project that was active at that time.
  4. Look at the chat messages sent then.

Often you will find a debugging loop or a big request you forgot about. Our guide on how to track your credit balance and history explains the history view in depth.

Step 5: Understand Why a Request Cost More

If one request deducted a lot of credits, remember that cost depends on the work done, not the length of your prompt. A short request can:

  • Touch many files
  • Create database tables and security rules
  • Run several attempts to fix its own errors

This is normal behaviour, not an error. Read why Lovable uses so many credits for the full list of causes.

Step 6: Check Your Purchase Details

If a purchase or top-up is missing:

  1. Check your email for a receipt.
  2. Make sure you bought credits for the correct workspace (people with several workspaces often buy for the wrong one).
  3. Check whether the payment was completed or still pending.

If you bought credits through a reseller, contact that seller with your order details first.

Step 7: Check Account Security

If you see usage at times you and your team were definitely offline:

  • Change your password immediately.
  • Sign out of other devices if the option exists.
  • Review workspace members and remove unknown people.
  • Never share your login with tools or people you do not trust.

Tools promising "unlimited credits" are a common cause of stolen accounts. Read more in does Lovable have unlimited credits.

Step 8: Contact Support With Clear Evidence

If none of the steps explain the difference, it is time to contact support. Include:

InformationWhy it helps
Your account emailIdentifies your account
Workspace nameCredits are per workspace
Date and time of the problemLets support find the exact entries
Screenshots of the balance and historyShows what you see
Receipt or order number (if a purchase)Proves the payment

Clear, organised evidence gets a faster answer. Be polite and specific; avoid only saying "my credits are gone".

How to Prevent This Next Time

  • Check your balance before and after big sessions.
  • Keep teammates in a separate workspace if possible.
  • Save receipts for every purchase.
  • Use strong passwords and never share your account.
  • Write precise prompts to avoid expensive loops.

What Happened to Imran

Imran checked his history and found that a teammate had used the shared workspace late on Friday night to fix a bug on another project. Nothing was missing; the credits were simply shared. He now uses a separate workspace for his personal projects, and his balance always matches his expectations.

Final Thoughts

When your Lovable credits seem missing or your balance looks wrong, go through the checklist calmly: refresh, check the period, check members, read the history, check purchases and security. Most cases are solved in minutes. For real errors, support can review your account when you provide clear evidence. If you need a trusted source for extra credits, visit our products page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why are my Lovable credits missing? The most common causes are teammates using a shared workspace, expired daily credits, large requests and display delays. Check your credit history first.

My purchased credits are not showing. What should I do? Refresh, wait a few minutes, check you bought for the correct workspace, and then contact the seller or support with your receipt.

Can Lovable deduct credits for a failed request? Credits are based on work done. If you believe a request clearly failed and was charged unfairly, contact support with the time, project and screenshots.

How do I stop others from using my credits? Review workspace members, remove people who do not need access and never share your login.
