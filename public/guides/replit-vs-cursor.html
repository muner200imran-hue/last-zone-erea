Quick verdict

Replit runs in your browser and hosts your app, so it's easier for beginners and quick projects. Cursor is a desktop editor for developers who want maximum control over their code and deploy it themselves.

Key differences that matter

  • Both have a $20/month entry plan (Replit Core, Cursor Individual).
  • Replit includes hosting and a database; Cursor doesn't host anything.
  • Cursor works with your existing projects and Git setup on your computer.
  • Replit works from any device with a browser, including tablets.

FAQ

Is Replit or Cursor better for beginners?

For people who can't code, the one that includes hosting and needs no setup is easier. Replit: Optional; you can read and edit code in a full editor. Cursor: Yes, you work directly in code.

Which is cheaper, Replit or Cursor?

Replit: Core $20/mo ($17 yearly) · Pro $100/mo ($95 yearly). Cursor: Individual from $20/mo · Teams $40/user/mo. Both have a free plan to try first.

Do I need hosting with Replit or Cursor?

Replit: Built in, including a database. Cursor: Not included; deploy elsewhere.
