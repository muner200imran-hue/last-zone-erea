One balance, five uses

Lovable uses one credit balance for: building (Plan and Build mode), chatting, Cloud hosting and backend, AI features inside your app, and generated images or videos.

Why some messages cost more

Cost grows with the amount of work: number of files changed, generated images, and new features like login. A vague prompt that makes the AI rebuild a page costs more than a precise one.

7 ways to make credits last

1. Plan first in Plan mode. 2. One clear request per message. 3. Name the exact page and element. 4. Don't repeat “fix it” — describe the error. 5. Use daily credits every day. 6. Use Visual edits for small text changes. 7. Read 12 ways to save credits.

Frequently asked questions

How do Lovable credits work?

Each message uses credits based on the work it does; one balance covers building, chat, hosting and AI features.

Do Lovable credits expire?

Daily credits expire each day, monthly plan credits after 2 months, top-ups after 12 months.

What is a credit on Lovable?

The unit Lovable uses to measure usage; its value depends on your plan and the feature used.

How many credits does a website take?

A simple landing page often costs a few credits; a full app with login and data can take dozens or more.
