Lovable Login Guide: Sign In, Fix Problems and Secure Your Account

Lovable login help: how to sign in to lovable.dev, fix common login problems, switch workspaces and keep your account secure.

How to sign in

Go to lovable.dev and choose Log in. Sign in with the same method you signed up with (Google, GitHub or email).

Common problems

Keep it secure

Never share login details, and be careful of anyone offering “shared accounts”.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I log in to Lovable?

At lovable.dev.

I forgot how I signed up

Try Google, GitHub and email in turn.

Can I share my account?

Invite members to a workspace instead.

Is there a Lovable app?

Lovable works in the browser on desktop and mobile.

Fix login problems step by step

  1. Make sure you use the same method you signed up with (Google, GitHub or email).
  2. Check your spam folder for the sign-in or reset email.
  3. Try a private browser window to rule out old cookies.
  4. Disable browser extensions that block scripts or cookies, then try again.
  5. If you still cannot get in, contact Lovable support from the official site.

Account security checklist

  • Use a unique password or sign in with a Google account protected by two-step verification.
  • Never share your login — invite teammates to your workspace instead.
  • Be careful with any extension or website that asks for your Lovable password.
  • Remove workspace members who no longer need access.

Login vs adding login to your app

Signing in to Lovable itself is different from adding sign-in to the app you build. For your app, ask Lovable: “Add email and Google sign-in; only signed-in users can see the dashboard.” See our guide to adding authentication.
