Plan your app on one page

  1. Problem: one sentence on what it solves.
  2. Users: who uses it and how often.
  3. Core screens: list no more than five.
  4. Data: what needs to be saved (users, bookings, products).
  5. Money: free, subscription, or one-time payment.

This page becomes your first prompt for an AI builder or your brief for a developer.

Launch checklist

  • Tested on desktop and on a real phone.
  • Privacy policy and terms pages.
  • Error messages that tell users what to do.
  • Analytics to see what people use.
  • A way for users to contact you.

After launch

Ask five real users to try it and watch where they get stuck. Fix the biggest problem first, then add features. Most successful apps grow from a small version that people actually use.
