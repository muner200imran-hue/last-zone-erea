Free Lovable Credits Explained

Free daily credits and paid monthly credits work differently. Compare limits, rollover, top-ups and expiry so you pick the right plan.

The core difference

Free credits are a small daily grant (5 per day, capped at 30 per month) that expire at the end of each day. Paid plans add a monthly pool of credits on top of the daily ones, and unused monthly credits can roll over while your subscription stays active.

Which ones are spent first?

On paid plans, daily credits are used before monthly ones, so steady daily use stretches your monthly pool further.

Which should you use?

Free credits suit experiments and small landing pages. If you regularly hit the daily limit mid-task or need a custom domain, a paid plan saves time. See Lovable pricing and Is Lovable free?.

Frequently asked questions

Do free credits roll over?

No, daily credits expire at the end of each day.

Do paid credits expire?

Unused monthly credits roll over while your subscription is active; check the official pricing page for current rules.
