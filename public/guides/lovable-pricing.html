Every plan, including Free, also gets a monthly grant of 20 Cloud credits (hosting and backend) and 4 AI credits (AI features inside your app).

What one credit actually buys

Lovable charges by how much work a message does, not a flat fee. Plan mode costs 1 credit per message; normal build messages vary. Official examples:

PromptWork doneCredits
“Make the button gray”Updates button styles0.50
“Remove the footer”Removes the footer0.90
“Add authentication with sign up and login”Adds login pages, logic and routes1.20
“Build me a landing page, use images”Landing page, 3 images, 5 sections1.70

So 100 Pro credits is roughly 100–200 small edits, or 60 bigger features — it depends heavily on how you write prompts.

Hidden costs people miss

Cloud hosting: a live app with a backend uses credits from the same balance once the 20-credit grant is used. AI features: if your app calls AI, that also draws credits. Fix loops: asking the AI to fix the same error repeatedly is the most common way people burn a month of credits in a day.

How to pay less

1. Use Plan mode (1 credit) to think before building. 2. Bundle small changes into one clear message. 3. Use the daily 5 free credits every day — they don't roll over. 4. Annual billing is usually cheaper per month. 5. Students and teachers can apply for Lovable's official education discount.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Lovable cost per month?

The Free plan is $0. Pro starts at $25/month with 100 credits, Business starts at $50/month, and Enterprise is custom.

Do unused Lovable credits roll over?

On paid plans, monthly credits stay usable for 2 months after they're issued. Daily credits never roll over.

Does Lovable charge per message?

It charges credits per message based on work done: Plan mode is 1 credit, build messages vary, for example 0.50 for a small style change.

Is hosting included in Lovable pricing?

Each plan includes a monthly 20-credit Cloud grant; heavier hosting draws from your normal credit balance.
