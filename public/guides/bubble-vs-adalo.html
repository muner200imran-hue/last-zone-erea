Detailed reviews

Bubble review

Visual no-code builder (drag and drop), with AI help

Strengths
  • Mature visual editor with a big community
  • Database, logic and hosting built in
  • Web and native mobile on one plan
Weaknesses
  • Steep learning curve
  • No code export — apps stay on Bubble

Best for: Non-coders who prefer designing screens and logic visually.

Adalo review

Drag-and-drop builder for mobile and web apps

Strengths
  • Simple drag-and-drop for mobile apps
  • App Store publishing on paid plans
  • Flat price, no usage surprises
Weaknesses
  • Performance limits on bigger apps
  • No code export

Best for: Founders who want a simple app in the App Store without code.

FAQ

Is Bubble or Adalo better for beginners?

On our beginner-friendliness score, Bubble gets 5/10 and Adalo gets 7/10. Bubble: No code, but there is a learning curve for the editor. Adalo: No code needed.

Which is cheaper, Bubble or Adalo?

Bubble: Starter $59/mo · Growth $209/mo · Team $549/mo (yearly). Adalo: Starter $36/mo · Professional $52/mo · Team $160/mo (yearly). Free options — Bubble: Free plan for building and testing; Adalo: Free: 500 records per app.

Do I need separate hosting with Bubble or Adalo?

Bubble: Built in, with its own database. Adalo: Built in, with database.

Can I use Bubble and Adalo together?

No — choose one.
