Quick verdict

This is less a contest than a fork in the road. Webflow builds beautiful websites. Bubble builds web apps with users, data and logic. Many startups use Webflow for the marketing site and Bubble for the product.

Detailed reviews

Bubble review

Visual no-code builder (drag and drop), with AI help

Strengths
  • Mature visual editor with a big community
  • Database, logic and hosting built in
  • Web and native mobile on one plan
Weaknesses
  • Steep learning curve
  • No code export — apps stay on Bubble

Best for: Non-coders who prefer designing screens and logic visually.

Webflow review

Visual website builder for designers and marketing teams

Strengths
  • Pixel-perfect design control
  • Strong CMS and SEO tools for marketing sites
  • Trusted by big companies
Weaknesses
  • Takes weeks to learn properly
  • Plans and pricing changed in 2026 and are complex

Best for: Designers and companies building polished marketing sites.

FAQ

Is Bubble or Webflow better for beginners?

On our beginner-friendliness score, Bubble gets 5/10 and Webflow gets 5/10. Bubble: No code, but there is a learning curve for the editor. Webflow: No code, but the editor is pro-level and takes time to learn.

Which is cheaper, Bubble or Webflow?

Bubble: Starter $59/mo · Growth $209/mo · Team $549/mo (yearly). Webflow: Basic from $15/mo (yearly) · Premium and Team plans above. Free options — Bubble: Free plan for building and testing; Webflow: Starter: free webflow.io site, 2 pages.

Do I need separate hosting with Bubble or Webflow?

Bubble: Built in, with its own database. Webflow: Built in, with CMS.

Can I use Bubble and Webflow together?

Yes — a common setup is Webflow for the website and Bubble for the app.
