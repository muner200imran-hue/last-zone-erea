Detailed reviews

Lovable review

AI app builder (chat in browser)

Strengths
  • Builds a full web app (pages, login, database) from plain chat
  • Hosting, domain and database included — publish in one click
  • Code syncs to GitHub, so you're never locked in
Weaknesses
  • Credits run out fast on big changes
  • Focused on web apps, not native mobile

Best for: Non-coders and founders who want a working web app fast.

Webflow review

Visual website builder for designers and marketing teams

Strengths
  • Pixel-perfect design control
  • Strong CMS and SEO tools for marketing sites
  • Trusted by big companies
Weaknesses
  • Takes weeks to learn properly
  • Plans and pricing changed in 2026 and are complex

Best for: Designers and companies building polished marketing sites.

FAQ

Is Lovable or Webflow better for beginners?

On our beginner-friendliness score, Lovable gets 9/10 and Webflow gets 5/10. Lovable: No coding needed; full code is yours. Webflow: No code, but the editor is pro-level and takes time to learn.

Which is cheaper, Lovable or Webflow?

Lovable: Lite $5/mo · Pro $25/mo (100 credits) · Business $50/mo. Webflow: Basic from $15/mo (yearly) · Premium and Team plans above. Free options — Lovable: 5 credits a day (up to 30 a month); Webflow: Starter: free webflow.io site, 2 pages.

Do I need separate hosting with Lovable or Webflow?

Lovable: Built in, with database, login and file storage (Lovable Cloud). Webflow: Built in, with CMS.

Can I use Lovable and Webflow together?

Yes — Webflow for the marketing site, Lovable for the app.
