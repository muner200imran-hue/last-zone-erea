FAQ

Is Framer or Wix better for beginners?

On our beginner-friendliness score, Framer gets 7/10 and Wix gets 9/10. Framer: No code needed. Wix: No code needed.

Which is cheaper, Framer or Wix?

Framer: Basic $10/mo · Pro $30/mo (yearly billing). Wix: Light $17 · Core $29 · Business $39 · Elite $159 per month (yearly). Free options — Framer: Free plan, Framer domain, 500 AI credits to try; Wix: Free plan with a Wix address.

Do I need separate hosting with Framer or Wix?

Framer: Built in, with CMS. Wix: Built in, plus domain and email options.

Can I use Framer and Wix together?

No need — they do the same job.
