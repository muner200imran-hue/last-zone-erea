FAQ

Is Webflow or Framer better for beginners?

On our beginner-friendliness score, Webflow gets 5/10 and Framer gets 7/10. Webflow: No code, but the editor is pro-level and takes time to learn. Framer: No code needed.

Which is cheaper, Webflow or Framer?

Webflow: Basic from $15/mo (yearly) · Premium and Team plans above. Framer: Basic $10/mo · Pro $30/mo (yearly billing). Free options — Webflow: Starter: free webflow.io site, 2 pages; Framer: Free plan, Framer domain, 500 AI credits to try.

Do I need separate hosting with Webflow or Framer?

Webflow: Built in, with CMS. Framer: Built in, with CMS.

Can I use Webflow and Framer together?

Rarely needed — they solve the same job. Pick one per site.
