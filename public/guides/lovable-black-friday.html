How to judge a Black Friday offer

  1. Check the source. Real offers appear on lovable.dev, Lovable’s official social accounts or inside your account — not in random DMs.
  2. Compare with annual billing. Annual plans are often cheaper than monthly, so a “deal” may be smaller than it looks.
  3. Count your real usage. Look at how many credits you used in the last two months before buying a bigger plan.
  4. Read the renewal price. Discounts often apply to the first period only.

What to do before the sale

  • Finish small projects on the Free plan (5 credits a day) so you know your needs.
  • Write down the features you will build next — a clear plan makes paid credits last longer.
  • Check whether you qualify for the student and teacher discount, which may beat a seasonal offer.
