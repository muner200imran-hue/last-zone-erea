Frequently asked questions

Can AI make a whole app?

Yes for many web apps.

Can AI make iPhone apps?

Some tools do; most AI builders make web apps.

Is it expensive?

Often cheaper than hiring for simple apps.

Do I need a developer?

Not to start; maybe later for complex needs.

StageWith an AI builder

Your job vs the AI’s job

  • You: decide what the app does, write real content, test with real users, and check privacy and payments.
  • The AI: writes the code, sets up pages and data, fixes errors you report, and keeps the design consistent.

Signs you need a developer too

Bring in a developer when you handle sensitive data (health, finance), need complex integrations, expect heavy traffic, or keep hitting the same bug. Exporting the code to GitHub lets a developer join without starting over.
