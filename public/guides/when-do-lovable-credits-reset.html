When Do Lovable Credits Reset? Daily, Monthly and Cap Rules

When do Lovable daily credits reset? Exact rules for daily credits, the 30-credit monthly cap, paid plan renewals and credit expiry, explained simply.

Daily credits

Every plan gets 5 daily build credits. They expire at the end of each day and do not roll over, so unused daily credits are lost.

The monthly cap

On Free, daily credits stop after 30 in a calendar month and resume on the 1st at 00:00 UTC. Convert to your time zone: that's 03:00 in Riyadh, 08:00 in Dhaka and 21:00 the previous day in São Paulo and Buenos Aires.

Paid plan credits

Monthly credits arrive with each billing cycle and stay usable for 2 months, so unused credits carry into the next month. Top-ups last 12 months.

Expiry summary

If you cancel

You keep remaining credits until the end of your billing period, then move to Free with only free grants.

Frequently asked questions

What time do Lovable credits reset?

The monthly cap resets on the 1st at 00:00 UTC; daily credits expire at the end of each day.

Why did my daily credits stop?

On Free you hit the 30-credits-per-month cap; they resume on the 1st.

Do paid credits reset to zero monthly?

No, monthly credits remain usable for 2 months after issue.

Do top-up credits expire?

Yes, 12 months after purchase.
