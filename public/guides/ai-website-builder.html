How AI website builders work

You describe the site in plain language — business type, pages, style, and what visitors should do. The builder writes the layout and text, shows a preview, and lets you refine it by chat or by clicking elements. The better tools also handle hosting, forms, a database and custom domains, so the site is live without extra services.

A five-step process that works

  1. Write a one-paragraph brief: who you are, who the site is for, main pages, and the main action (call, book, buy).
  2. Paste your real content — services, prices, address — instead of placeholders.
  3. Review each page on desktop and phone.
  4. Add a contact or booking form and test it yourself.
  5. Publish, connect your domain and submit the sitemap to Google Search Console.

Questions to ask before choosing

  • Can I export or own the code?
  • Are custom domains included in my plan?
  • Can I edit text without spending AI credits?
  • Does it support separate pages with their own titles for SEO?
  • Can it store form submissions or user accounts if I need them later?
