How to Add Stripe Payments to a Lovable App

Selling a subscription, a product or a one-time service? Lovable can build your pricing page and checkout. Here are the two ways to do it — and how to stay safe.

Pro+for built-in payments
Test firstbefore real money
Neverpaste keys into the chat

Option 1 — Built-in payments (simplest)

  1. Ask in chat, e.g. “Add a pricing page with a $29/month subscription.”
  2. Lovable guides you through turning on a provider, creating products and prices, and building checkout.
  3. Test the whole flow in the preview.
  4. Finish the go-live checklist in the Payments tab before accepting real payments.

Built-in payments need Pro or higher, and only project or workspace admins and owners can set them up.

Option 2 — Your own Stripe account

  1. Ask Lovable to add payments using your own Stripe account.
  2. Lovable shows a Connect Stripe form.
  3. Create a restricted key in your Stripe dashboard and submit it in that form — never in the chat.
  4. Start with a test key. A live key can charge real cards even from the preview.

Subscriptions vs one-time payments

Subscriptions need user login so each payment is tied to an account. One-time payments can work without accounts. Add login first if you plan to sell memberships.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add Stripe on the free plan?

Built-in payments need Pro or higher. On Free you can connect your own Stripe account.

Where do I put my Stripe key?

Only in the Connect Stripe form Lovable shows — never in the chat.

Can I test without real money?

Yes. Use a Stripe test key and test cards first.

Do I need login for payments?

For subscriptions, yes. One-time payments don't require accounts.

Who can set up payments?

Project or workspace admins and owners.
