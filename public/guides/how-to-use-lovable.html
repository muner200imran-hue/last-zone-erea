How to Use Lovable: Beginner Tutorial From First Prompt to Publish

How to use Lovable step by step: create a project, write prompts, use Plan mode and visual edits, connect a database, and publish your app.

Steps 1–4

1. Sign in at lovable.dev. 2. Type your idea on the home page. 3. Watch the preview build. 4. Ask for changes one at a time.

Steps 5–8

5. Use Plan mode (1 credit) to plan big features. 6. Use Visual edits for text and colours. 7. Add login and data with Lovable Cloud. 8. Click Publish.

Frequently asked questions

Is Lovable easy?

Yes, it's designed for non-coders.

How do I publish?

Click Publish in the top right.

Can I undo changes?

Yes, from History.

Can teams use it?

Yes, invite members to a workspace.

Example: a booking app in one afternoon

  1. “Create a booking site for a hair salon with Home, Services with prices, and a Book page.”
  2. “On the Book page, add a form for name, phone, service, date and time. Save each booking.”
  3. “Add an Admin page that lists bookings, newest first. Only I can sign in to it.”
  4. “Send me to a Thank you page after booking.”
  5. Check on your phone, then press Publish.

Common first-week questions

Can I change the design later? Yes, any time. Is my work saved? Every change is saved as a version. Can someone else help? Invite them to your workspace or connect GitHub.
