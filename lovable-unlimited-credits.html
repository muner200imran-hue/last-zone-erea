Straight answer

Lovable unlimited credits: the honest answer

A lot of people arrive here hoping we will say yes. We will not, because it would not be true, and a false promise is a bad way to start a relationship with a customer.

What follows is why the phrase exists, what it usually hides, and what genuinely does help when your credits keep running out too soon.

Why unlimited is not possible

Every credit corresponds to real computing work being done on your behalf, and that work has a cost that someone has to pay. A seller offering unlimited credits is either paying that bill out of their own pocket — which no business does for long — or they are not giving you what they claim.

We sell credit packages with clear numbers instead: you know what you bought and what it cost.

What "unlimited" listings usually are

  • A shared account, which stops working when someone else drains it or the owner changes the password.
  • A cracked build that reads what you type in your browser.
  • A very small allowance described with very large words.
  • A short promotion that quietly ends after your first session.

What we actually offer

Credit packages of a stated size, attached to a key with a stated device limit and daily switch allowance. Nothing hidden, nothing implied. If you need a larger package than the ones listed, ask and we will price it.

How to get far more from the credits you have

Describe the outcome, not just the problem

A clear request is answered once. A vague one is answered two or three times, and you pay for all of them.

Ask for small, targeted changes

Correcting two lines is dramatically cheaper than regenerating an entire page because two lines were wrong.

Start fresh threads for new topics

Very long conversations get more expensive with every reply, because the whole history is re-read.

Plan before you build

Ten minutes of planning routinely saves more credits than any discount will.

Frequently asked questions

So there is no unlimited option at all?

Correct. We sell packages with a stated number of credits, and we will always tell you the number.

What if I need more than the biggest package?

Message us. Larger amounts can be arranged; they just are not unlimited.

Are the sellers advertising unlimited credits scams?

We cannot speak about specific sellers, but the promise itself is not something the underlying economics support.

What is the single biggest credit saver?

Asking for narrow, specific changes instead of full rewrites.
