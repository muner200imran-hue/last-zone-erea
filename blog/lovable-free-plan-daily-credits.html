Lovable Free Plan: How Many Free Credits Per Day and When They Reset

LAST ZONESeptember 24, 2026

Rahim wants to build a small portfolio site. He signs up for Lovable, builds a hero section, adds a contact form and then, halfway through the afternoon, sees a message saying he has no credits left for today. He asks the same question thousands of people search every month: "Is Lovable free, and how many free credits does Lovable give?"

This guide explains the Lovable free plan in plain words: how the daily credits work, what the free credit limit means, when Lovable free credits reset, and how to squeeze real value out of the Lovable free tier.

Is Lovable Free?

Yes, you can use Lovable for free, with limits. The free tier is designed so anyone can try the platform, build small projects and learn how it works before paying.

What you get on the free plan typically includes:

  • A small number of free credits that refresh regularly.
  • Access to the core builder so you can create and edit projects.
  • The ability to preview your app while you build.

What you do not get is unlimited usage. Heavy builds will reach the limit quickly.

How Many Free Credits Does Lovable Give?

Lovable gives free users a limited daily allowance of credits, often with a monthly cap on top. The exact numbers can change as Lovable updates its plans, so always check the pricing page inside your account for the current amount.

What matters more than the exact number is how you use it:

  • A few small, precise edits can fit comfortably in one day.
  • One large feature (for example, login plus a database) may use most of a day's free credits.
  • Debugging loops can use the whole daily allowance quickly.

Lovable Daily Credits: How They Work

Lovable daily credits are a fresh small allowance you receive each day on the free plan.

Key points

  1. They refresh on a schedule, not the moment you run out.
  2. Unused daily credits usually do not roll over to the next day.
  3. A monthly limit may also apply, so daily credits cannot be used forever without limit.

Because unused daily credits normally disappear, the smart move is to use a little each day instead of saving everything for one big session.

When Do Lovable Free Credits Reset?

This is one of the most common questions. Free credits reset on a fixed daily schedule, based on a set time rather than your local midnight. That is why some users feel their credits "reset at a strange hour".

How to find your reset time

  1. Open your workspace settings.
  2. Go to Plans & Credits.
  3. Look for the next reset or renewal information.

Tip: Note the reset time in your phone. Plan your biggest prompt of the day just after the reset, so you have the full allowance available.

Lovable Free Credit Limit: What Happens When You Hit It

When you reach the Lovable free credit limit:

  • You cannot send new AI build prompts until the reset.
  • Your project is safe and nothing is deleted.
  • You can still view and preview your work.
  • You can upgrade or wait for the next reset.

For a full list of options, see what happens when Lovable credits run out.

How to Get the Most From the Lovable Free Tier

1. Plan before you prompt Write your page list and features in a note first. Then send one clear prompt for the structure instead of five vague ones.

2. One feature per prompt Small focused requests are cheaper and less likely to break something that then needs fixing.

3. Do simple edits yourself Changing a word or a colour? Use the visual editor or code view where possible instead of asking the AI.

4. Avoid "fix it" loops If something breaks, describe the problem precisely: what you clicked, what you expected and what happened. This fixes issues in fewer attempts.

5. Build daily Because daily credits usually do not roll over, a short session every day gets you more total work than one big weekly session.

More tips are in our full guide: how to save Lovable credits.

Can I Use Lovable for Free Forever?

You can keep a free account and use the daily credits as long as the free tier exists. For small hobby projects that is often enough. For anything larger, most people eventually upgrade because the free credit limit slows down development.

Final Thoughts

The Lovable free plan is a genuine way to learn and build small projects. Understand your daily credits, know when they reset, and use precise prompts. Rahim, from our example, now plans his prompts the night before and finishes one feature per day without hitting the limit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Lovable free to use? Yes. Lovable has a free tier with a limited number of credits so you can build and test small projects.

How many free credits does Lovable give per day? Lovable gives a small daily allowance on the free plan. The exact number can change, so check the Plans & Credits page in your account for the current amount.

When do Lovable free credits reset? Free credits reset on a fixed daily schedule. You can see the next reset time in your workspace billing or credits settings.

Do unused daily credits carry over? Usually no. Unused daily credits normally expire at the reset, so it is best to use them every day.
