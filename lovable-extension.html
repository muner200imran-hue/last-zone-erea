Lovable Extension: what it is and how it works

If you build projects with Lovable, most of your day is spent inside one browser tab. The Lovable extension from LAST ZONE sits in that tab and gives you a small control panel of its own — your credit balance, your server choice, your project invite link and your support shortcuts, all without leaving the page you are working on.

This page explains exactly what the extension does, who it suits, which browsers it runs in and what you get when you buy a license key. If you are comparing options, start here and then follow the links to the more specific pages further down.

What is the Lovable extension?

The Lovable extension is a browser add-on that you install once and activate with a license key. After activation it shows a compact panel with the information you normally have to hunt for: how many credits you have left, how many device switches you have used today, which server your session is using and where to get help.

It is not a separate app and it does not replace Lovable. It is a companion layer for the browser you already work in, built so that the numbers you care about are one click away instead of several pages deep.

Who is it for?

  • Solo builders who ship small projects and want to see their balance before starting a long session.
  • Agencies and freelancers who hand work over to clients and need a clean invite link at hand.
  • Teams that share one license across a small number of machines and need to know how many device switches remain.
  • Anyone who has lost track of their credits mid-build and had to stop halfway through a task.

The problem it solves

The most common complaint we hear is not about features at all. It is about surprises — a build stopping because credits ran out, or a login refusing to work because the daily device switch limit was already used up on another computer.

Both problems are caused by missing information, not by bad luck. When the balance and the switch count are visible while you work, you plan around them instead of discovering them at the worst moment.

Supported browsers

On a computer the extension runs in any modern Chromium-based browser. On a phone, Chrome does not allow extensions, so we recommend Quetta Browser, which does.

  • Credits are consumed as you use them — a license is not an unmetered allowance, and we say so plainly.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Lovable account as well?

Yes. The extension works alongside your own Lovable account; it does not create or replace one.

Is the extension on the Chrome Web Store?

No. You download the file from the LAST ZONE homepage and load it into your browser, which is why the install steps are written out for you.

Can I use one key on two computers?

You can move between machines within your key's device limit and daily switch allowance. Beyond that the key will refuse the new device until the next day.

What happens when my license expires?

The panel stops activating. Your Lovable account and projects are untouched — you simply renew the key to carry on.
